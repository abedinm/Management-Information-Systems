# Case 1: UPS Competes Globally with Information Technology

Chapter 1, *Spotlight on Technology Case Study* (Laudon 18th ed., pp. 18–19). Source: [`MIS18_Case_1.pdf`](MIS18_Case_1.pdf)

## Key facts

- Founded 1907. Today it has 500,000+ employees, 125,000+ vehicles and 557 aircraft, and delivers about 25 million packages a day to 220+ countries.
- Spends **$1 billion a year on IT** to keep service high and costs low.
- **Package tracking:** a bar-coded "smart" label → data sent to computer centers (Mahwah NJ, Alpharetta GA) → the driver's **DIAD** handheld captures signatures and pickup/delivery data → central network → tracking on the website, the app and for customer service. Handles about 743.5 million tracking requests a day.
- **AI systems:**
  - **DeliveryDefense:** predicts whether a delivery will succeed, so at-risk shipments can be rerouted to a UPS Store or Access Point and package theft is reduced.
  - **Network Planning Tools (NPT):** reroutes packages around storms and handles unusually large shipments.
  - **ORION:** optimizes drivers' routes. It has saved about 100 million miles and 10 million gallons of fuel.
