## Rules worth remembering

- **No makeup quizzes.** Don't miss one.
- **No late case assignments.** The case and due date are announced in class. You analyse it with the competitive forces or value chain model.
- **Poster:** A2, made digitally and printed on PVC paper, with the title and member names in the lower corner.
- **Makeup exams** need written permission from the Dean, Chairman or BBA Program Coordinator, and the questions are harder than the originals.
